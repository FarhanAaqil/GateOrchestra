# Chat assistant evaluation

Generated by `python scripts/chat_eval.py analyze` from `results/chat_eval/raw.jsonl`.

80 of 80 questions (`chat_eval/questions.jsonl`: facts, arithmetic,
multi-step questions and Python output) were answered by the real chat pipeline twice, once
answering directly after the quick check and once researching with tools first. Gates are scored
from those two runs with the verdicts each gate gave on the same turn.
Models: probe groq:openai/gpt-oss-20b, answer groq:openai/gpt-oss-120b, agent groq:qwen/qwen3.8-27b.

## A gate trained on chat questions

Not trained: only 1 question(s) where research helped; too few to learn from.

## What it means

Recommended default for the chat's routing: **Always answer directly** (the cheapest option within one question of the best accuracy, the same rule the benchmark gate uses). Research fixed 1 question(s) that answering directly got wrong and broke 0, at about 2.8 times the tokens. The gate trained on the benchmark escalated 0% of these questions and scored 98.8%, the same as answering directly: it does not spend research where research rarely helps. A chat-specific gate cannot be trained from so few cases where research helps; that needs harder or longer questions than these.

These are 80 questions with short, checkable answers, so they say how often the assistant gets such answers right and what research costs; open-ended requests (explanations, writing) are not graded here.
